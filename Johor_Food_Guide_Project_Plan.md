# Johor Eat, Drink, Play, and Enjoy

Reference project: https://github.com/timqian/chinese-independent-blogs

### How to Submit

1. Add a row to the end of [./food-original.csv] with the restaurant's name, cuisine/type, average cost/price range, rating (out of 5), Amap URL, business hours, contact/reservation details: whether booking is needed, waiting time, tag (budget eats or high-end), and remarks.
2. Submit a PR
3. (Automatically) After the PR is merged, README is generated via [./readme_render.py]

## Why This List?

The idea behind this food guide is simple. For those of us living abroad for years, the time we can spend back home each year is as fleeting as a cicada's wing. Because time is precious, we don't want to settle for just any meal. I needed a list that would help me rediscover the flavours of home, day by day, during my short visits.

Beyond personal use, I also hope that this collection of culinary delights can offer a warm reference to anyone currently living in Johor or planning to visit this charming city.

#### A Few Thoughts Before You Dive In:
As the old saying goes, "One man's meat is another man's poison." Tastes vary, and food is all about personal connection. This guide inevitably carries my own life experiences and preferences, so it may not resonate with everyone. If even one recommendation strikes a chord with you, that will be the greatest reward for this effort.
